## 🏆 The conclusion up front

**Plan: Aditya Birla Activ One MAX + Chronic Care add-on — one individual policy per parent.**

The [Health study](../Health/README.md) rated day-1 chronic cover *"largely inert"* for a healthy 26-year-old and picked HDFC. **For these parents that verdict inverts:** three of their four conditions are exactly ABHI's day-1 chronic list, and only ABHI covers them from day one. That single feature is close to decisive.

### Why ABHI here (the son's #2 becomes the parents' #1)

| Factor | Why it matters for the parents |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Day-1 Chronic Care** | Waives the 3-yr PED wait → **0 days** for BP, diabetes, cholesterol (+ asthma, COPD, obesity, CAD). On every other plan these wait **36 months**. |
| ✅ **No co-payment** | Many senior plans force a 10–20% co-pay; ABHI MAX has none. |
| ✅ **No max entry / exit age** | Father (60) enters cleanly. |
| ✅ **PED reduction add-on** | Cuts the 3-yr wait to **2 or 1 year** — the lever for the one gap (fatty liver). |

### The one gap to plan around: fatty liver

Fatty liver is **not** on the day-1 chronic list, so it waits out the PED period like on any plan. Mitigation: **buy the paid PED-wait-reduction add-on** (→ 1–2 years) so its clock runs as short as possible. Because fatty liver can progress (NASH → cirrhosis), starting that clock **now** matters.

### Hard avoids for these conditions

- 🚫 **Care Supreme** — its wording permanently excludes illness *"attributable to"* alcohol (clause 4.2.24), a discretionary denial lever aimed squarely at fatty liver / cirrhosis. See [Health/care_supreme M2](../Health/policies/care_supreme/module2_exclusions.md).
- 🚫 **ACKO** — 37 limits delegated to a per-policy schedule; too much uncertainty for high-claim-probability lives.
