# 💰 Premium (Zone 3), underwriting & the buy checklist

## Zone: Gaya 823001 → ABHI Zone 3 (cheapest)

ABHI prices by **where the insured resides**, not where they're treated:

| ABHI zone | Cities | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 (dearest) | Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Gurgaon, Thane, Navi Mumbai | — |
| Zone 2 | Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad, Noida, Chandigarh | — |
| **Zone 3 (cheapest)** | **Rest of India — incl. Gaya & Patna** | ✅ **Both parents** |

> Since the parents reside in **Gaya (823001)**, they are priced in **Zone 3** regardless of the son being in Bangalore. Only if they *relocate* to Bangalore would Zone 1 pricing apply — and ABHI's §5.18-style continuing-disclosure duty means an address change must be notified.

## Indicative premium ranges — planning figures, NOT quotes

⚠️ **ABHI publishes no senior rate chart, and four declared PEDs mean the real price comes only from underwriting.** These are **order-of-magnitude bands** from market norms + ABHI's one public anchor (age 25 / ₹15L / Zone 1 = ₹10,149). PED loading (**~25–100%**) is folded into the range. **Do not treat as a quote.**

| Structure (per parent) | Mother (54), Zone 3 | Father (60), Zone 3 |
|---|---|---|
| **B · ₹10L base + ₹40L top-up** + Chronic Care | ~₹28,000–42,000/yr | ~₹38,000–58,000/yr |
| **A · ₹50L single base** + Chronic Care | ~₹42,000–62,000/yr | ~₹58,000–85,000/yr |

**Both parents, all-in (indicative):**
- Structure **B ≈ ₹66,000 – ₹1,00,000 / yr**
- Structure **A ≈ ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,50,000 / yr**

**Offsets:** up to **₹50,000** deductible under **Section 80D** (senior-parent limit, **old tax regime** only — nil/limited under the new regime). Individual health premiums are **GST-exempt** since 22 Sep 2025.

## The underwriting reality (because everything is declared)

1. **Pre-policy medical tests are near-certain** at these ages with these conditions — ABHI bears the cost, but budget the time.
2. **Expect a premium loading** (25–100%) and possibly a **condition-specific waiting or carve-out**, most likely on the **fatty liver**. Get any counter-offer **in writing** before paying.
3. **Declare all four conditions fully and honestly** — non-disclosure is the #1 cause of denied senior claims, and ABHI's remedy ladder (exclude / add waiting / load) beats an ab-initio cancellation, but only if you disclosed.

## Buy checklist (before payment)

- [ ] Confirm the schedule names **Activ One MAX** (not a cheaper capped sibling under the same UIN)
- [ ] Confirm **Chronic Care add-on** is attached (day-1 for BP/diabetes/cholesterol)
- [ ] Add the **PED-wait-reduction add-on** (→ 1–2 yr) for the **fatty liver**
- [ ] Confirm **zero co-pay** on the schedule (no voluntary co-pay modifier silently attached)
- [ ] Confirm **room at actuals** (no room-rent cap → no proportionate deduction on long ICU stays)
- [ ] If Structure B: buy **base + top-up same insurer, same day**; get the **top-up's chronic/PED terms in writing**
- [ ] Verify **Super Reload** covers **same illness** + its **2nd-claim trigger**
- [ ] Declare **all four conditions**; keep every acknowledgement
