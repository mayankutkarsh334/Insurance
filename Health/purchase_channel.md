# 🛒 Purchase Channel — how to actually buy the policy

_Researched 29 August 2026 · applies to the [HDFC Optima Secure+ decision](decision_record.md)_

> The study answers *what* to buy. This answers *through whom* — which turns out to matter for reasons that have nothing to do with price.

---

## 💸 Price is not the variable

Health premiums are **IRDAI-filed** per product / variant / age / zone. Intermediary commission is paid by the insurer **out of that premium**, not added on top.

> **The same plan at the same SI costs the same direct, through a broker, through a corporate agent, or through an agent.** There is no channel discount to shop for. Declining an intermediary does not save money — it just redirects a commission you pay regardless.

The **21%/18% Favourable Claims Experience Discount** ([M4](policies/hdfc_optima_secure/module4_cost.md)) is a **product** feature, not a channel perk.

---

## ⚖️ The intermediary taxonomy — who may sell what, and who they work for

| Type | Insurers they may represent | Legally represents | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual agent** | **One per category** | ⚠️ **The insurer** (contractual duty) | A local HDFC ERGO agent |
| **Corporate agent** | **Three per category** (9 total: 3 life, 3 general, 3 health) | ⚠️ **The insurer** | **Ditto** |
| **Broker** | ⭐ **Whole of market** | ⭐ **You** (fiduciary duty) | PolicyBazaar's broking entity; local IRDAI brokers |
| **Insurer direct** | Its own products only | The insurer | hdfcergo.com |

> 🚩 **An individual agent cannot give a cross-market opinion — not through dishonesty, but by regulation.** They have nothing else to sell. Asking one "what should I buy?" is asking the one channel structurally incapable of answering.
>
> 💡 **But that bias can be used deliberately:** meet an HDFC ERGO agent *and* an Aditya Birla agent separately and ask each *"why yours over theirs?"*. With two finalists 0.05 apart, an adversarial pair is worth more than another neutral survey. Neither will produce a balanced view; together they produce the prosecution case for each side.

## 🎛️ What the channel *does* control — the schedule

These are **not** in the wording. They are configured per policy, by the issuing channel:

| Clause | Effect | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **§2.12 PED reduction** (36 → 24/12 mo) | Shortens the plan's one real weakness | Wording: *"allowed to be opted at **channel level only**… Policyholders will therefore **not** be able to opt for the same."* **You cannot ask for it — a channel might carry it. Ask every channel.** |
| **§2.13 room-rent modifier** | Re-enables proportionate deduction | Must **NOT** be applied |
| **Variant naming** | "Optima Secure **+**" vs cheaper capped-room siblings under the same UIN | Schedule must name the right rung |
| **Instalment premium** | Claim while on EMI → remaining instalments netted off payout ([M6](policies/hdfc_optima_secure/module6_finprint.md)) | Take **annual** |

> ⭐ **This is the real reason the channel matters.** The price is fixed; the *configuration* is not.

### Business model — why the advice is free

```
  Finshots newsletter  ──►  warm, educated audience  ──►  near-zero CAC
                                                              │
                                     free 30-min advisory ◄────┘
                                                │
                              commission from insurer on purchase
                                                +
                       RENEWAL COMMISSION for as long as the policy lives
```

- **Revenue = commission + renewal commission**, paid by the insurer out of the premium.
- **The Finshots funnel is the answer to "why free"** — customer acquisition costs a fraction of what a firm buying ads and running outbound call centres pays.
- **No outbound sales team.** An expensive thing not to have, and real evidence the model is what they claim.
- They publicly **refuse to recommend ULIPs** — the fattest commissions in the industry.

### Alignment and conflict — honestly

| ✅ Aligned | 🚩 Conflicted |
|---|---|
| **Renewal commission is an annuity that only pays while the policy stays in force** — a lapsed, ported, or claim-denied policy kills it. For a 40-year hold, about as good as commission structures get | **The firm earns only if you buy.** Nobody gets paid if the right answer is *"you don't need this"* |
| **Advisors are salaried**, not paid per sale — removes the *individual's* steering incentive | **Commission rates differ by insurer.** They say they rank by their own rating, not commission — unverifiable from outside |
| Legally required to **disclose commission in writing** at point of sale | **Six-insurer panel** — cannot recommend outside it |
| | **Reportedly HDFC-ERGO-heavy** (longest partnership, most policies handled) — so **their agreeing with this study's pick is weak evidence** |

> **Weight them above a commission-paid agent, below a fee-only adviser who takes no commission at all** (e.g. Beshak, or a fee-only planner) — the only genuinely conflict-free structure, and the one you pay for directly.

## 🔵 Decision

> **Ditto by video call first (blind), then PolicyBazaar in person (blind), then a second informed Ditto call.**

- Price is identical, so the choice is about **advice quality and schedule configuration**, not cost.
- Ditto's salaried advisors and renewal-commission alignment make it the better *advisory* sample; PolicyBazaar provides the in-person meeting and a differently-biased second read.
- **Use a secondary phone number for PolicyBazaar.**
- **Give no PAN, Aadhaar, or payment details at the opinion stage** — none are needed for advice.
