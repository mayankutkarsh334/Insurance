What moves the price — age, cover size, features & health

A visual read across the seven deep-studied plans of how the yearly premium is actually built: it climbs with age, scales sub-linearly with sum insured, shifts with the features you switch on, and can be loaded for a health condition. Then: what cover to buy at each age.

01

Three laws of the premium

Everything on this page follows from these. They hold for every plan in the study, not just one.

LAW 1 — AGE

Cheap decade, then a J-curve

1 → ~10×

What ₹1 costs at 26 costs roughly 3× at 55 and 10× at 75. Bands are near-flat in your 20s–30s; the cliff lands after 60 (+40%/band).

LAW 2 — SUM INSURED

Bigger cover, cheaper per rupee

₹1,763 → ₹355

Per lakh of cover at 26 (Bajaj): ₹10L costs ₹1,763/lakh, ₹1Cr just ₹355/lakh. Doubling cover adds ~25–35%, not 100%.

LAW 3 — YOU

Features add, health can multiply

up to ×2.5

Riders/SI move the base a little; a declared health condition can load the premium up to +100–150% — for life. Buying young & healthy avoids it.

Why a bigger cover is cheaper per rupee

The single most decision-relevant fact for a young buyer. Premium is sub-linear in sum insured — a fixed policy cost is spread over more cover. Bars show ₹ of premium per ₹1 lakh of cover at age 26. Lower is more efficient.

Premium per ₹1 lakh of cover · age 26

Bajaj & SBI verified across all SI tiers; ABHI estimated; HDFC/Care shown at ₹15L, ACKO at ₹1Cr (their published anchors).

ACKO sells ₹1 crore for about what HDFC/Care charge for ₹15 lakh (₹162 vs ~₹890 per lakh) — it is direct-to-consumer with zero agent commission. But that same ₹162/lakh is the arithmetic behind its weak claims payout (57.8% ICR): cheap cover and low rupee-payout are two views of one number.
05

How premium moves with your health

Base premium is for a standard, healthy life. Declare a condition (high BP, high BMI, diabetes, a past surgery) and the insurer can apply an underwriting loading — a percentage surcharge on top of the chart, usually permanent, applied at entry and every renewal. This is separate from, and stacks on, age and SI.

Two honest reads. (1) A stated ceiling (HDFC, SBI) is better than silence — Bajaj, ABHI, Care & ACKO leave the maximum open-ended. (2) For a healthy 26-year-old the expected loading is zero on every plan — which is exactly why the loading table is an argument for buying now: a condition diagnosed at 40 can attach a +50–150% surcharge that then rides your premium for the rest of your life. None of these plans can raise your price simply because you claimed (claim-based renewal loading is banned) — the lever is health disclosed at entry.

07

What to buy at each age

Cover need, and which features earn their keep, shift across a lifetime. The through-line: buy a large SI young — it is cheaper per lakh and ages more gently, and it front-loads waiting-period credit before any health condition can attach a loading.

20sAge 25–30 · buy now

Lock the cheap band and go big on cover

You are in the flattest, cheapest decade and fully insurable with no loading. This is the one time a large SI is nearly free per rupee. Prioritise an uncapped bonus / restore so the cover compounds while it's cheap; skip wellness gimmicks and PED features you don't need.

30sAge 30–40 · consolidate

Top up the sum insured before health drifts

Still a cheap band, but marriage/dependants and early lifestyle risks (BP, BMI) appear. Raise SI to ₹50L–₹1Cr now while an SI increase won't trigger a loading. Confirm the bonus engine is compounding and consumables/day-care are covered.

SI ₹50L–₹1CrConsumables buy-backMaternity (if planned)Wellness earn-back
40sAge 40–50 · the last easy window

Fix cover high — the curve starts to bite

Bands now step +12–23%. Any new condition here attaches a permanent loading, so finalise your maximum SI before that happens. PED-waiting reductions become genuinely relevant. Prefer a plan with no contractual escalator — an 8%/yr printed rise compounds brutally over the decades ahead.

50sAge 50–60 · manage the cliff ahead

Protect affordability into retirement

Every band from 61 adds ~40–44%. Max out accrued no-claim bonus now (it's free cover), and consider a base + super top-up structure so a smaller base carries the age-loading while a top-up supplies the crore. A flatter-curve / zone-agnostic plan (SBI Infinite) ages most gently.

60+Age 60+ · the expensive years

Optimise for what old age actually uses

Premium may triple between 60 and 75. Value shifts to OPD, domiciliary, day-care and consumables, and to low/zero co-pay. Never let an old policy lapse — its waiting periods and no-loading history are irreplaceable at this age. Lifelong renewability (all seven have it) is now the whole point.
